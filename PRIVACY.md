# Photo Sorter — Privacy Policy

Photo Sorter is made by Tudor Andrei-Cristian. It runs entirely on your computer.

- **No servers, no accounts, no telemetry.** We don't collect, receive or store any data about you or your photos. There are no analytics and no crash reports.
- **Your photos stay where you put them.** The app reads the folders you open and copies the photos you favourite into the session folders you choose. It never modifies or deletes your originals; removing a favourite moves only the app's own copy to your system's trash.
- **What the app stores:** your sessions, settings and the last opened folder, in one file in your system's application data folder. It never leaves your computer.
- **Network use:** at most once a day the app asks GitHub (github.com) whether a newer version exists, and downloads it if so. That request reveals your IP address and the app version to GitHub, under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Nothing else is sent.
- **Cloud folders:** if you choose a favourites folder inside Google Drive for desktop (or a similar sync client), that client uploads the files. Photo Sorter has no access to your Google account.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/andrei399/photo-sorter/issues.
